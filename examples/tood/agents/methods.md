# Calibration and assumptions

Check the definition of the score, its direction, and its aggregation across tasks.
Distinguish a common monotonic score transformation from a change in sample ranking.

Inspect calibration data, replay memory, reference statistics, and parameter selection.
Check whether the method requires information available at deployment. Account for
small calibration sets and degenerate scale estimates only where the source supports it.

Assess whether the stated intervention can address the claimed failure mechanism.
Separate effects on logit scores from effects on feature geometry. Credit explicit
limitations and controlled comparisons when they support a bounded conclusion.

Return at most the task's candidate limit. Each candidate needs exact evidence.
These topics guide inspection. They are not predetermined findings about TOOD.
